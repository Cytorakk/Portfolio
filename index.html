The Hybrid Narrative

Science meets Syntax

I don't just write code; I understand the biological data flowing through it. As a Medical Laboratory Scientist, I have been trained in precision, quality control, and the critical importance of accurate diagnostics.

As a Developer, I translate that rigorous discipline into structured, bug-free applications. I noticed a gap in digital resources for lab students and professionals — which led me to build LabVerse.

My goal is to digitize the laboratory experience, making medical education accessible, interactive, and efficient through modern web technologies.
